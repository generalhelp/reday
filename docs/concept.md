# RE:DAY — Concept

## Elevator pitch

RE:DAY is a browser-based interactive narrative in which the player relives the same day repeatedly. Every loop resets the world, while the player keeps what they have learned.

The core fantasy is simple:

> What would you do differently if tomorrow started exactly like today — but you remembered everything?

## Target audience

The project is aimed at ordinary web users, including people who do not normally consume games, interactive fiction, or creative-industry content.

The experience should be understandable without tutorials, gaming knowledge, or social features.

## Experience goal

RE:DAY should create curiosity about creative industries by letting the user experience the output of multiple creative disciplines working together.

Instead of explaining what screenwriters, illustrators, sound designers, game designers, and developers do, the product demonstrates their combined effect through an interactive story.

## Core loop

1. Wake up at 07:00.
2. Move through a sequence of scenes.
3. Make choices.
4. Discover information.
5. Reach the end of the day.
6. Reset.
7. Use remembered information to unlock new actions.

## Key design idea: knowledge as progression

Traditional games often use levels, coins, items, or experience points.

RE:DAY uses knowledge.

A player may learn:

- a person's motivation;
- a hidden relationship;
- a door code;
- the timing of an event;
- the reason behind an argument;
- where someone will be later in the day.

A later scene can test whether this knowledge is present and reveal a new option.

## Tone

The intended tone is cinematic, human, slightly mysterious, and emotionally grounded.

The product should not feel like a quiz, educational portal, or social network.

## Ending philosophy

There should be no universal “perfect day.”

Different players can prioritize different people and outcomes. The final screen summarizes the route they created rather than assigning a moral score.
